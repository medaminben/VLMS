VLMS, the Virtual Library Management System, is the library's desk application. It keeps the catalogue, the members, and the loans, and it is the place where a book is added, a member is registered, and a copy goes out and comes back.

## The screens

- [Catalogue](catalogue) — titles, copies, and local numbers.
- [Members](members) — who may borrow, and until when.
- [Circulation](circulation) — what is out, what is late, and what has come back.
- [Archive](archive) — what was removed, and what can still be restored.
- [Metrics](metrics) — a short picture of the library.

The ? beside the theme button opens this manual.

## Where to start

Read [Getting started](getting-started) once. The three jobs that come up every day are [Lend a book](task-lend-book), [Return or extend](task-return-extend), and [Register a member](task-register-member).

## How this manual is organised

The first pages describe a screen each. The task pages are the steps for one job. The [Reference](reference) holds the rules that several screens share: [loan states](reference#loan-states), [member status](reference#member-status), and the [local number](reference#local-number).
