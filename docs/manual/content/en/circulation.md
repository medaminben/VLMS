Circulation is the live list of loans. Archived loans are on the [Archive](archive) screen.

![Circulation, with the filters, search, pictures, and buttons numbered.](shot:circ-overview)

1. The status filter.

6. The member's photograph, or `No photo`.
7. `Return`

The details of the highlighted loan sit under the two pictures: the member, the copy, and the dates.

## The status filter

The filter is one choice. It opens on `All`.

- `Open` — out, and due today or later.
- `Overdue` — out, and past the due day.
- `Returned` — back on the shelf.

The three states are exclusive. They are set out in [Loan states](reference#loan-states).

![Overdue loans only.](shot:circ-overdue)

## The member filters

Under the status filter are the lists from [Members](members): status, sex, age group, and city keep the loans whose member matches. The year list, which starts on `All loan years`, is the year the loan was made, not the year the member registered. They combine with the status filter and with each other, so `Overdue` and `Youth` lists the late loans of young members.

## Search

Search matches the book's title, the membership number, or the member's full name.

![Circulation after a search by the member's name.](shot:circ-search-name)

## The buttons

`Checkout` lends a copy. `Extend` and `Return` follow the highlighted loan and are available only while that loan is still out. `Delete` archives a returned loan. The steps are [Lend a book](task-lend-book), [Return or extend](task-return-extend), and [Remove](task-remove-book).
