The Catalogue is the live list of titles. Archived titles are on the [Archive](archive) screen, under Books.

![The Catalogue, with its filters, search, columns, and buttons numbered.](shot:cat-overview)

1. Categories. `All categories` leaves every category in the list. Pick one or more to narrow it.
2. Languages. `All languages`, or Arabic, French, and any other language the catalogue holds.
3. Cover. `All`, `With cover`, or `Without cover`.
4. Search, by title, author, ISBN, or local number.
5. `Local no.` The number the cell is speaking for. See [Local numbers](reference#local-number).
6. `Copies` and `Available`. Copies is every live copy. Available is the ones still on the shelf.
7. The details of the highlighted title: cover, description, and the bibliographic fields.
8. `Loans`
9. `Add Book`
10. `Edit`
11. `Delete`

## Search

Type in the search box. The list narrows as you type. A local number that matches a copy exactly is preferred. A prefix of a number is used only when no copy has that number exactly, so a year such as a title still finds the title.

![A search by local number. The matched number leads the cell.](shot:cat-search-number)

## The local-number column

One copy shows its number. Several copies show the number and how many further copies the title has. Click the cell to open the drop-down. Each number is green on the shelf, or red and italic when that copy is out.

![The local-number drop-down of a title with several copies.](shot:cat-number-dropdown)

Choosing a number marks it in the closed cell. The copies stay in their original order, and the choice lasts until the list is loaded again.

![Green for a copy on the shelf, red and italic for a copy on loan.](shot:cat-copy-colours)

The colour rules are in [Local numbers](reference#local-number).

## Loans

`Loans` follows the highlighted title. It opens even when the title has never been borrowed, and it offers `Checkout` from there. The list is every loan of every copy the title has ever had, including copies and loans that have since been archived.

![The loans of one title.](shot:cat-loans-dialog)

`Close` leaves the dialog. Lending from here is [Lend a book](task-lend-book). Adding a title is [Add a book](task-add-book). Removing one is [Remove](task-remove-book).
