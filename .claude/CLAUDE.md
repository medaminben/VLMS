# VLMS

Follow [CONTRIBUTING.md](../CONTRIBUTING.md). In short:

- Work on a feature branch and open the pull request against `Beta`. Never push to `main` or `Beta` directly.
- Do not create or push `v*` tags, and do not change version numbers. Merging into `main` releases automatically.
- Commit subjects go into the release notes. Write one sentence in the imperative, starting with a capital and ending with a full stop, for example `Fix the due date shown after a renewal.` Do not use `feat:`-style prefixes.
