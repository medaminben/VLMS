Home

What VLMS is, and where to begin.

VLMS, the Virtual Library Management System, is the library's desk application. It keeps the catalogue, the members, and the loans, and it is the place where a book is added, a member is registered, and a copy goes out and comes back.

The screens

  • Catalogue — titles, copies, and local numbers.
  • Members — who may borrow, and until when.
  • Circulation — what is out, what is late, and what has come back.
  • Archive — what was removed, and what can still be restored.
  • Metrics — a short picture of the library.

The ? beside the theme button opens this manual.

Where to start

Read Getting started once. The three jobs that come up every day are Lend a book, Return or extend, and Register a member.

How this manual is organised

The first pages describe a screen each. The task pages are the steps for one job. The Reference holds the rules that several screens share: loan states, member status, and the local number.
